FAQ

Common Questions

Do you charge for travel?

Travel is free within 20 miles of Merced. Beyond that, it's $1 per mile. No hidden fees!

What ID do I need?

Valid government-issued photo ID: driver's license, passport, military ID, or state ID card.

How quickly can you come?

Same-day appointments are often available. Call (209) 230-4390 for urgent requests within the hour.

Do you work weekends and holidays?

Yes. I'm available 24/7, including weekends, evenings, and all holidays.

Can you notarize at hospitals or jails?

Absolutely. I specialize in bedside signings and detention facility visits with proper clearance.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, Venmo, Zelle, and all major credit/debit cards. Payment is due at time of service.
